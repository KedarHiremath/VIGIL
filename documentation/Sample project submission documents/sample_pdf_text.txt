Farming yield is also largely dependent on diverse and multiple soil parameters, like Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium, Crop cycle, Soil moisture, soil pH, temperature and climatic aspects like temperature, rainfall, etc. Nowadays India is very rapidly progressing towards technical innovation and development. Thus, technology will be beneficial as well as blessing to agriculture which will enhance crop productivity which will result in better yields to the farmer.  The proposed project provides an innovative solution for Smart Agriculture by monitoring the soil nutrients and climatic conditions which can assist farmers in increasing productivity to a great extent. This project presents a system, in a form of a website, which uses Machine Learning techniques in order to predict three most suitable crops based on soil parameters and climatic conditions and also most profitable crop based on the predicted future market price. This system integrates the data collected from repository, Weather department and by applying machine learning algorithm a prediction of most suitable crops according to current environmental conditions is made. This will provide farmers with variety of options of crops that can be grown with enhanced yield. Thus, the project develops a system by integrating data from various sources, data analytics, prediction analysis which will improve crop yield productivity and ultimately increase the profit margins of farmers helping them over a longer run.  Agriculture  Different types of soils have various mineral contents and every crop require multiple mineral components for its better growth. Therefore an agronomist should be known about the type of soil he has so that he could cultivate better crops. Another important issue that is famers have to face is the uncertainty in the market demand and prices from the time of sow to the time of harvest.  Machine Learning  Machine Learning is a section of Computer Science where new developments evolve at recent times, and also helps in automating the evaluation and processing done by the mankind, thus reducing the burden on the manual human power. It provides unique model which performs joint prediction of both rainfall and soil features on the yield, which is also termed as hybrid model. The model reads historic data and learns through computational processes like ARIMA, Exponential Sequencing and Recurrent Neural Networks. It accepts soil features, fertilizer concentration and sowing period from the end user and provides expected yield in tonnes per hectare as output for given parameters. The approach not only promises precise levels of expected yield but also helps determines seasonality trends in the yield of the same crop over different years in the same season. The proposed model is trained only for historic district-wise rainfall data and soil data of Karnataka state and predicts the yield of rice for same.  Crop Selection Method to Maximize Crop Yield Rate using Machine Learning Technique [IEEE 2015]  An agro-based country depends on agriculture for their economic growth. When the population of a country increases dependency on agriculture also increases and the subsequent economic growth of the country is affected. In this situation, crop yield rate plays a significant role in economic growth of the country. In addition to the biological approaches (eg. Seed quality of crop, crop hybridization) and some chemical approaches (eg. Use of fertilizer, urea, potash) approaches a crop sequencing technique is required to improve net yield rate of crop over season.   This paper proposed a new and unique method named Crop Selection Method (CSM) to achieve net yield rate of crops over season. This technique classifies the crops in 4 categories as Seasonal crops (eg. Wheat, paddy), Whole year crops (eg. Vegetables, paddy, toor), Short time plantation crops(eg. Potato, vegetables,  rese), and long-time plantation crops (eg. Sugarcane).Algorithm provides unique combination of these crops that can be selected in a sequence based on yield rate per day as output. Method may improve net yield rate of crops using limited land resources and also increases re-usability of the land.  Crop Yield Prediction and Efficient use of Fertilizers [IEEE 2019]  The system provides a model precise in predicting the crop yield and also recommendations about the required fertilizers ratio based on soil parameters and atmospheric conditions to maximize the crop yield and farmers revenue. Among the factors for better crop yield, use of fertilizers is also an important factor as it directly affects the fertility of soil. Early detection of problems and providing their solutions will help in better crop yield. This paper predicts the crop yield based on two algorithms (Random Forest Algorithm and Back Propagation Algorithm) and compares the result of two algorithms so that the algorithms with more accuracy and less errors can be used. Also, the system provides the end user with the proper fertilizer ratio to be used to get the desired crop yield.  Decision Making Support System for Prediction of Prices in Agricultural Commodity [IEEE 2019]  Agriculture commodity prices have a variable nature impacting the profit negatively. Agriculture price prediction has always been a challenging problem for farmers. Farmers are unable to get the desired price for their crops due to which suicide rate is increasing year by year. Different social and economic factors are responsible for fluctuation of prices of agriculture commodity. This paper provides a decision making support model for prediction of prices in agriculture commodity which will help in reducing agribusiness risks. This paper uses soft computing techniques for predicting prices of various crops. Using data mining algorithms paper provides average price prediction for agriculture commodities helping farmers in decision making and ultimately profit making. Graphical comparison of prices of agriculture commodities is provided. The decision model takes previous sell price from farmer and predicts the future price.  Prediction of Future Market Price for Agricultural Commodities [International Journal of System and Software Engineering 2015]  Agricultural items have seasonal prices. These prices are spread over the year. If these prices are predicted in advance then it will be promising to the farmer on Return on Investment (ROI). The prediction of future market price of price may be valuable for budgetary planning systems for different government agencies. It will help in growth of economy of country like India where main source of income for larger population in agriculture. This paper provides a system which alerts the farmers about the crop he/she can sow such that by the time of harvesting he/she gets better return on investment. The market price of the crop from the time of sowing and harvesting can be uncertain. This paper makes use of neural networks (Neuroph framework) and back propagation algorithm for prediction of future price of the crops. The suggested model has the prediction engine which is the heart of the entire model. The paper provides the results in only graphical format and predicts the prices of only five crops (wheat, bajra, rice, paddy and jowar)  CROP PREDICTION USING PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS [IEEE 2017]  Theme:  In this paper, the author mainly focuses on aggregating various papers and further utilizing them to develop a model to predict suitable crops, crop yield and recommending fertilizers (future scope).  Model:  NPK sensors will be utilized to sense the nutrients data from soil.
